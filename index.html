ABOUT US

A one-man studio, close attention, maximum discretion

HORAK MOTION was founded by me, Jan Horak. I love movies, and I found my passion in both shooting and editing video — two very different but equally important sides of filmmaking. In my opinion, it works best when one person handles both, without having to explain his vision to someone else for the final cut. Every project passes through the same hands, from the first frame to the final export.

Forget big cameras and other clunky gear. We shoot with just a small pocket camera (DJI Osmo Pocket 4 — first photo) and a drone (DJI Mini 5 Pro — second photo). That keeps our prices very competitive and our presence at every event very discreet. Of course, we'll sometimes direct people to capture a memorable shot, but nobody will be tripping over our equipment. With a set of ND filters, careful camera settings and color grading in the final cut, we deliver footage that feels genuinely cinematic. Don't hesitate to book us for a video of your event, property or business that feels like a Netflix movie. We work throughout Andalucía, and travel costs are included in our prices.

I'm a certified drone pilot and follow Spanish regulations for drone filming. If you'd like drone shots included in your video, please book at least 14 days ahead so we have time to obtain the necessary flight authorisation.
